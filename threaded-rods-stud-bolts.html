Threaded Rods & Stud Bolts

Threaded rod is made from round bar as per the specified grade and diameter. Standard diameter and grade threaded rod will typically be mass produced by a thread rolling machine. This process involves passing round bar through two hardened steel rollers to imprint the thread onto the round bar.

Stud Bolts are externally threaded fasteners without a head, normally used with 2 Hex nuts & 2 Flat washers on either sides as an assembly. Stud bolts are prominently used in flange connections and the diameter and length varies as per the flange dimensions.

Types of Threaded Rods & Stud Bolts

  • Fully Threaded Stud( End to End type & Thread to Thread type)
  • Tap-End Stud
  • Double-End Stud
